About Our Club

The UNC Table Tennis Club is dedicated to promoting the sport of table tennis among students, faculty, and staff at the University of North Carolina. We welcome players of all skill levels, from complete beginners to competitive players.

Our Mission

To foster a vibrant community of table tennis enthusiasts, provide opportunities for skill development, and create lasting friendships through our shared passion for the sport.

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Do I need to be experienced to join?

No! We welcome players of all skill levels. Whether you're a beginner or advanced player, you'll need to try out to join the team, but our tryouts are open to everyone.

❓ What do I need to bring?

Just yourself! The club provides tables and basic equipment. You can bring your own paddle if you prefer, but it's not required.

❓ Is there a membership fee?

Yes, membership is $20 per year. This helps us maintain our tables, organize events, and support club activities.

❓ Where do we play?

We practice at the Underground at the Student Union. Check our schedule for practice times!

❓ How can I join the club?

Attend one of our practice sessions and participate in a tryout! No previous experience needed - we're looking for dedicated players of all levels.

❓ What events do you host?

We host fun events like Pizza and Ping Pong nights, social gatherings, and friendly competitions with other schools including the NCSU Table Tennis team!
